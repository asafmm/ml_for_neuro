How it is scored

  • Each submission predicts all 403 test trials: 13 participants × 31 lotteries.
  • We rank by test MSE. Lower is better.
    RMSE = √MSE, in the same units as the choices (shekels).
    R2 = 1 − MSE / variance of the test choices.
    Above 0 means the model beats predicting one number for everyone.
  • Each team keeps its best week.
    The lowest test MSE across the whole competition wins 5 bonus points.
  • The weekly deadline is Saturday at 23:59.
    Only the last submission before it is scored. Later ones count for the next week.
  • Teams marked reference are simple models of ours.
    They show what a basic model scores.

Remember. The final grade comes from the oral exam, and there we care about how well you understand your model, much more than about where you rank!
